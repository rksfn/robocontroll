Camera calibration
  1. Put a small mark (tape, coin) on the table where the camera can see it.
  2. Jog the arm (pitch about 90°, gripper pointing down) until the gripper tip just touches the mark.
  3. Click the mark in the camera image. Repeat for 5-8 marks spread over the whole reachable table area.
Four points is the minimum; more points spread wider gives better accuracy. Table height is taken from these points and becomes the arm's safe floor.
